FAQ

Straight answers.

Are you an incubator or accelerator?

No. Syndicate Holdings Group is the holding company for our brands, Creator Ops Media and Syndicate Marketing. We help founders and businesses bring ideas to life through the services those brands offer.

Do you invest in ideas or startups?

No. We don’t offer capital, and nothing on this site is an offer to invest. What we bring is experience: strategy, branding, marketing and TikTok Shop know-how.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need. TikTok Shop consultations with Grady are $150 per hour. Marketing and branding work from Syndicate Marketing is quoted based on the project. The first conversation about your idea is a chance to find out whether we’re a fit.

Will you keep my idea confidential?

Please don’t send anything secret in your first message. Ideas aren’t treated as confidential unless we’ve both signed a confidentiality agreement. Our Terms of Use explain why. Start with a short summary, and we can talk about an agreement if it makes sense.

Can my business work with one brand only?

Yes. Work with Syndicate Marketing for marketing, Creator Ops Media for TikTok Shop consultations, or both.

Where are you based?

Syndicate was founded in Spartanburg, South Carolina. We don’t have a headquarters. We work remotely and meet clients where they are.

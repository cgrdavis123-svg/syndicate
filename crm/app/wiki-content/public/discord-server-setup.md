**{{name:com-discord-setup}}** ({{price:com-discord-setup}}) builds a Discord server around your game, stream, team or business. It works for a brand-new server or a rebuild of one that grew messy.

## What's included

{{features:com-discord-setup}}

## How it goes

1. **Kickoff call.** We talk through who the server is for, what members should do there, your tone and your rules.
2. **Server plan.** We send the channel list, roles and rules to your client portal for approval.
3. **Build.** We set up channels, permissions, onboarding, AutoMod and bots, and test it all with a test account.
4. **Walkthrough.** A short call with you and your moderators to show how everything works.
5. **Changes and handover.** One round of changes, then the staff guide in your portal.

## Who owns it?

You do. If we create the server for you, we transfer ownership to you at handover. See [[discord-access]].

> **Good to know:** Some server features, like a banner or custom invite link, need Discord Server Boosts. We'll tell you if anything you want depends on them.

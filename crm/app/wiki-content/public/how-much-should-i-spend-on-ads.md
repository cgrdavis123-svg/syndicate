There’s no single right number, and anyone who promises a result for a fixed budget is guessing. We help you choose a starting budget at kickoff based on:

- **Your area.** A 10-mile radius costs less to cover than a whole state.
- **Your competition.** Some searches (like emergency plumbing or lawyers) cost more per click than others.
- **What a customer is worth to you.** If one job is worth $2,000, you can afford more per lead than if it’s worth $50.
- **What you’re comfortable testing** for the first two or three months.

## How we keep it under control

- Spend stays close to the monthly budget you set. Your ads portal shows a pacing bar, and we’re alerted if spending runs ahead.
- After the first month or two, we look at your **cost per lead** and move money toward what works.
- You can raise or lower the budget any time; just message us.

> **Heads up:** Small budgets can still work, but results take longer to read because there’s less data. We’ll tell you honestly if a budget is too small to be useful.

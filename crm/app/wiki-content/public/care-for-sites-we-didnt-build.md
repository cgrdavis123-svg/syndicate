Yes. Choose a care plan on its own at {{store}}. At checkout we ask for your website’s address, what it’s built with, and whether you have the admin logins.

## What happens next

1. **Monitoring starts the moment you pay**: uptime checks, the weekly security scan and the PageSpeed test.
2. **We get access.** You add us as a user (we’ll show you how). If you don’t have your logins, we’ll help you find them.
3. **We get set up**: a first backup or export, [[what-is-site-stats|Site Stats]] for visitor and speed numbers, and fixes for anything urgent the first scan finds.
4. **Then it runs every month.**

## Good to know

- Fixing problems that were there before you joined can use your edit time, or we’ll quote larger repairs first.
- On hosted platforms (Shopify, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow) the platform runs software updates and server backups; we export your content monthly instead.

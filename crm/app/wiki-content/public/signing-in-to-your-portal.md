Your client portal has no password to remember.

1. Go to {{portal}}.
2. Enter the email address we have for you.
3. We email you a **sign-in link**. It works once and expires in 30 minutes.

You stay signed in on that device for 30 days.

## Didn’t get the email?

- Check your spam or promotions folder for an email from {{email}}.
- Make sure it’s the email you ordered with. For security, the page doesn’t say whether an address has access.
- Still nothing? Email us and we’ll check.

## Ads clients

Your ads results, approvals and reports are in your **ads portal**. Sign in the same way at {{adsportal}}, or use the **Your ads** button inside your client portal.

## Want to look around first?

Open the [demo client portal](https://crm.stusyn.com/portal-demo.php). It’s a made-up business with sample data, so you can click through projects, approvals, invoices, website care and stats. Nothing you do there is saved or sent.

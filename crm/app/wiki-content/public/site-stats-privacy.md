---
title: Does Site Stats use cookies? Do I need a cookie banner?
category: analytics
order: 20
summary: No cookies, no personal data and no IP addresses stored.
keywords: privacy, cookies, gdpr, ccpa, banner, consent, tracking
---
Site Stats is built to respect your visitors’ privacy:

- **No cookies** and nothing stored on visitors’ devices.
- **No IP addresses** are stored. Visitors are counted with a code that changes every day and can’t be traced back to a person.
- **No cross-site tracking**, and we never sell or share the data.
- Bots and crawlers are filtered out.

Because it doesn’t use cookies or collect personal data, Site Stats itself doesn’t need a cookie banner. Other tools on your site might (ad pixels, embedded videos, chat widgets), so we check your whole site with you at launch.

> **Note:** This isn’t legal advice. If you have specific obligations, like customers in the EU or California, ask a lawyer what applies to you.

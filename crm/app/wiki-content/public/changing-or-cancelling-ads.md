## Changing your budget

Message us in {{adsportal}} with the new monthly amount. We adjust within one business day.

## Adding or switching platforms

Message us. Moving between one, two and three platforms changes your monthly plan price from your next bill.

## Pausing for a busy or quiet season

Tell us the dates and we’ll pause or scale the campaigns.

## Cancelling

Cancel any time in {{plans}}. Ads management needs **30 days’ notice**: your plan ends on the first billing date at least 30 days after you cancel, so you may be billed once more inside those 30 days. The exact end date is shown before you confirm, and we email you a confirmation.

When it ends, we pause the campaigns and remove our access. Your ad accounts, ads and data stay yours.

1. Sign in to {{portal}} and open **Website care**.
2. Under **Request a change**, say what you’d like changed. Add the page address and any details: new wording, prices, hours.
3. Send it. We get it straight away.

We start within **two business days** on the care plan, or **one business day** on Care Plus, and email you when the status changes: in progress, done, or if it needs a word with you first.

## Sending photos or files

Add them to your project in the portal and mention it in the request. For big files like video, a Google Drive or Dropbox link works best.

## What counts as an edit

Small changes to what’s already there: text, prices, hours, photos, a new section, a blog post formatted for you. New pages or a redesign are bigger jobs; we quote them first. See [[edit-minutes]].

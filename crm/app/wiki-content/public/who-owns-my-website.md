Once your order is paid in full and the work is delivered, **the website design, written content and logo files we create for you are yours**.

- Your **domain**, **hosting**, **ad accounts** and **data** are kept in your name from the start.
- Third-party software, fonts and platforms stay under their own licenses.
- We may keep copies for our records and, unless you ask us not to, show the work in our portfolio. See [[portfolio-and-confidentiality]].

If you ever move to another designer, we’ll hand over what’s yours. See [[ownership-and-your-accounts]].

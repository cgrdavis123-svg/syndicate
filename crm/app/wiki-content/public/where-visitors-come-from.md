Every visit is labelled with where it came from:

| Channel | Means |
|---|---|
| **Direct** | Typed your address, used a bookmark, or came from an app that hides the source |
| **Organic search** | Clicked a normal Google or Bing result |
| **Paid search** | Clicked a Google or Bing ad |
| **Organic social** | Clicked a normal post on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and so on |
| **Paid social** | Clicked a social media ad |
| **Local listing** | Came from your Google Business Profile |
| **Referral** | Clicked a link on another website |
| **Email** | Clicked a link in an email |

## Campaigns

When a link includes **UTM tags** (like `?utm_source=facebook&utm_campaign=spring-sale`), Site Stats shows that campaign by name. We add these to every ad we run, so you can see exactly which campaign brought each lead. See the [[glossary]].

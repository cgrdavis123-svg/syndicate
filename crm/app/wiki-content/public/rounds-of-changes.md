## What a round is

We send you a draft. You send back **one list of everything** you’d like changed: wording, photos, colors, layout. We make all of it. That’s one round.

Gather feedback from everyone on your side before you send it; a single combined list goes further than several small ones.

## After the rounds are used

Small fixes we got wrong never count against your rounds. If you want more changes after launch, a [[care-plans-compared|care plan]] includes monthly edit time, or we’ll quote larger changes first.

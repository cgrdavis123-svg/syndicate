The clock starts once we have what we need from you: your logo, photos, text and any logins (see [[what-we-need-from-you]]). We give you the exact dates at your [[the-kickoff-call|kickoff call]].

## What speeds it up

- Sending your content early. Waiting on content is the most common delay.
- Replying to drafts within a couple of days.
- **[[rush-delivery|Rush delivery]]** ({{price:add-rush}}), which moves your project to the front of the queue.

## What slows it down

If we don’t hear from you for 60 days during a project, we may close it. The work done so far is still yours. See our {{terms}}.

## Every 15 minutes

We check your website is up and answering. Two failed checks in a row counts as **down**, and we get alerted. See [[if-your-website-goes-down]].

## Every day

Your **security certificate** (the padlock). We’re warned 14 days and 3 days before it expires.

## Every week

- Your **domain registration** date. We’re warned 30 days and 7 days before it expires.
- A **security and health scan**: secure connection (HTTPS), browser security settings, private files that shouldn’t be public, insecure content, and up to 60 links checked for being broken.
- A **Google PageSpeed** test on a phone. See [[pagespeed-scores-explained]].

Everything shows on your **Website care** page in {{portal}}, in plain words.

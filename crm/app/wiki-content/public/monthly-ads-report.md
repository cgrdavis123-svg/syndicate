Early each month we email you a report on the month before. It opens as a web page you can print or save as a PDF.

It includes:

- **The numbers** against last month: spend, leads, cost per lead and clicks.
- **How the month went**, written by us in plain words, and **what we’ll do next month**.
- Spend and results **by day**, **by platform** and **by campaign**.
- **What we did**: launches, budget moves, new ads, tracking fixes.

Past reports are always in {{adsportal}} under **Monthly reports**, and the current month shows “so far”. Every plan also includes a monthly check-in call.

Site Stats is the visitor dashboard we include with every website and ads plan. It replaces tools like Google Analytics with one clear screen.

## What it shows

- **Visitors and page views**, live and over time.
- **Where they came from**: Google, Maps, social media, ads, email, other websites. See [[where-visitors-come-from]].
- **Leads**: phone taps, email clicks, form sends and purchases, counted automatically. See [[goals-calls-and-forms]].
- **Pages**: which ones people read, and how far they scroll.
- **Devices, browsers and locations** (country and, where available, region and city).
- **Speed** from real visits. See [[speed-numbers-explained]].

## Where to see it

In {{portal}}, under your website. You also get a summary by email early each month (or the fuller [[monthly-care-report|care report]] if you’re on a care plan).

We don't claim to be online 24/7. Here's what coverage actually looks like.

## Check-ins

- **{{name:com-manage}}:** we check in every weekday. Reports and member issues are handled within one business day.
- **{{name:com-manage-plus}}:** we check in every day, weekends included, and we recruit and train volunteer moderators from your community for extra coverage.

## Between check-ins

AutoMod and moderation bots run all the time. They block spam, scam and phishing links, slow down raids, and log everything so we can follow up.

## Urgent problems

For a raid or a compromised account, your staff guide shows how to lock the server down in one step, and you can reach us at {{email}}.

## Whose rules?

We moderate to your server's rules and to Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, including its minimum age. We report serious issues to Discord's Trust & Safety team, as Discord asks.

## What we won't do

We never buy members or use fake accounts or activity, and we don't promise member numbers. We make your server welcoming, safe and active; growth depends on your game, stream or brand.

> **Tip:** Want a change in how we moderate? Tell us in your portal and we'll adjust the rules and AutoMod settings.

Your website packages cover design and building. **Your domain name and web hosting are separate**, and they’re always in **your own name**, so you always own them.

## Want us to handle it?

Add **{{name:add-domain-setup}}** to your order. It’s {{price:add-domain-setup}} with the One-page and Starter websites, and **free with the Business website and Online store**. We use **Namecheap**, and we:

- set up a Namecheap account in your name (you keep the login),
- register your domain, or connect the one you already have,
- set up hosting that fits your package, with a security certificate (https),
- create your business email, like you@yourbusiness.com, and the settings that help it land in inboxes instead of spam,
- hand over every login with a short walkthrough.

So there are two payments: you pay Namecheap for the domain, hosting and email, on your own card, at their price. We don’t resell them or add a markup. You pay us once for the setup work, the part that’s easy to get wrong. A .com is usually about $10–20 a year, and Namecheap shows its current hosting and email prices before you buy.

## If you already have a domain

Great. At kickoff we’ll ask you to log in to where you bought it (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Squarespace and so on) so we can point it at your new website. We never move it out of your name.

## If you’d rather do it yourself

That’s fine too. We’ll tell you exactly what to buy and walk you through pointing it at your site at kickoff.

## Online stores

Online stores may run on a store platform with its own monthly fee instead of regular hosting. We’ll talk through the options at kickoff.

- **Your website, written content and logo files** are yours once your order is paid in full and delivered.
- **Your domain, hosting, ad accounts, Google Business Profile and data** are set up in **your** name from the start. We’re added as a user, never the owner.
- **Ad spend** is billed to you by the platforms.
- If you leave, we hand over what’s yours and remove our access.

Third-party tools (themes, fonts, plugins, store platforms) stay under their own licenses. See [[who-owns-my-website]].

No. There are two separate costs:

| | What it is | Who you pay |
|---|---|---|
| **Our fee** | Planning, building and running your ads, reports and calls | Us, monthly |
| **Ad spend** | What the platform charges when your ads show or get clicked | Google, Meta or TikTok, directly |

Your ad spend is billed to **your own card** by the platform, from **ad accounts in your name**. We never mark it up or handle it, and you can see every cent in the platform’s billing page.

You set a monthly ad budget with us at kickoff, and we keep spending close to it. Your ads portal shows spend against budget every day. See [[how-much-should-i-spend-on-ads]].

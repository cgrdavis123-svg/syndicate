- **{{name:web-business}}** and **{{name:web-store}}** sites are set up so you can edit text, blog posts and products yourself. We show you how at launch.
- **{{name:web-one-page}}** and **{{name:web-starter}}** sites are built for speed and simplicity, so we make changes for you.

## The easy way

A [[care-plans-compared|care plan]] includes edit time every month. You ask from your portal, we make the change and tell you when it’s done. See [[requesting-edits]].

Bigger changes, like new pages or a redesign, are quoted first so there are no surprises.

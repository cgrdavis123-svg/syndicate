Every project starts with a kickoff, where we learn what we need to do great work for you. For a website you can pick how:

## The kickoff form (websites)

Your order email and your client portal have a link to a short form. It takes about 10 minutes:

- What your business does, your services, and who your customers are.
- Where you work, your hours, and why customers pick you, in your own words.
- The pages you’d like, colors and websites you like, and anything to avoid.
- **What to show on your site**: the phone number, email, address and links you choose. Anything you leave empty stays off the site.
- Your logo and photos, if you have them. No photos yet is fine.

We start on your website as soon as you send it, and you can change your answers until we start building. Want to talk too? Tick “I’d also like a kickoff call”, and we start right after the call instead.

## The kickoff call

A free 30-minute video call. It’s the usual start for ads and other services, and any website client can book one instead of (or as well as) the form. We cover:

- Your goals, your customers and what makes your business different.
- What we need from you: logo, photos, text, logins. See [[what-we-need-from-you]].
- Your domain name and hosting, if it’s a website. See [[domains-and-hosting]].
- Ad accounts, budget and the area to target, if it’s ads. See [[give-us-access-to-ad-accounts]].
- Dates: when you’ll see the first draft and when we’ll launch.

Your confirmation email has a private link to book it at any open time.

> **Good to know:** Your payment is fully refundable until your kickoff: the call, or sending the form if you skip the call. See [[refunds]].

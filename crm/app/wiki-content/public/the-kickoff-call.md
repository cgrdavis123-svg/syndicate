Every project starts with a free 30-minute kickoff call. It’s where we agree on the details so the work goes smoothly.

## What we cover

- Your goals, your customers and what makes your business different.
- What we need from you: logo, photos, text, logins. See [[what-we-need-from-you]].
- Your domain name and hosting, if it’s a website. See [[domains-and-hosting]].
- Ad accounts, budget and the area to target, if it’s ads. See [[give-us-access-to-ad-accounts]].
- Dates: when you’ll see the first draft and when we’ll launch.

## Booking it

We email you within one business day of your order to book it, and your confirmation email has a private link to book it yourself. Pick any open time.

> **Good to know:** Your payment is fully refundable until the kickoff call. See [[refunds]].

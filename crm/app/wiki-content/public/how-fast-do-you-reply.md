We reply to emails, contact forms, portal messages and help center questions **within one business day**. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding US public holidays.

## If something’s urgent

- **Your website is down?** If you’re on a care plan, we already know: our monitoring checks your site every 15 minutes and alerts us. See [[if-your-website-goes-down]].
- **Ads spending more than expected?** Message us in {{adsportal}} and put “Urgent” at the start.
- Otherwise, email {{email}}.

## Change requests

Care plan edit requests are started within two business days on the care plan, or one business day on Care Plus. See [[requesting-edits]].

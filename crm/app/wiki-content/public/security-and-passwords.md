---
title: How do you handle access and passwords?
category: policies
order: 30
summary: We ask to be added as a user, not for your password.
keywords: security, passwords, access, login details, phishing, safe
---
- **We don’t ask for your passwords.** Wherever we can, we ask you to add us as a user or partner (Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, WordPress, Shopify and so on), so you can remove our access any time.
- If a service has no way to add a user, we’ll explain why we need a login and how to change it afterwards.
- We never ask for card numbers by email or phone.
- Our own systems use two-step sign-in, and secrets are stored encrypted.

> **Heads up:** We’ll never email you a link asking you to “verify” a payment or sign in somewhere unexpected. If something looks off, forward it to {{email}}.

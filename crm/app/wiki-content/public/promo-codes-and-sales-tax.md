## Promo codes

If you have a code, enter it on the Stripe payment page, in the **Add promotion code** box under your order. The discount shows before you pay. Codes can’t be added after an order is paid.

## Sales tax

If sales tax applies to your order, it’s shown on the payment page before you pay and listed on your receipt. Questions about tax on a specific order? Email {{email}}.

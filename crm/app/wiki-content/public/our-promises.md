## What we promise

- **Published, fixed prices.** What you see on {{store}} is what you pay.
- **A reply within one business day.**
- **Nothing goes live without your approval**, whether it’s a website or an ad.
- **Honest reporting.** You see what was spent and what came of it, including when something didn’t work.
- **Your accounts and data stay in your name.**
- **Cancel online any time** (see [[cancel-a-plan]]).

## What no honest agency can promise

We can’t guarantee rankings, traffic, leads or sales. Those depend on your market, budget and the platforms’ own rules. Be wary of anyone who does.

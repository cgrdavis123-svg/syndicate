We never need to be the owner of your server. Here's how access works.

## If you already have a server

1. After you order, we'll send our Discord username to your client portal and by email.
2. Invite that account to your server.
3. Give it a staff role with **Manage Channels**, **Manage Roles**, **Manage Messages**, **Moderate Members** and **Kick/Ban Members**. For server setup we also need **Manage Server** while we build.
4. Let us know in your portal, and we'll take it from there.

## If we're building the server for you

We create it, build it, then **transfer ownership to you** at handover. We keep a staff role only if you have a monthly plan.

## Keeping it safe

- We use two-factor authentication on our account, and we turn on the two-factor requirement for moderators on your server.
- We never ask for your Discord password.
- You can remove our role or our account at any time.

## When a plan ends

When a plan ends, we remove our account from your server. If you'd like us to leave sooner, just ask.

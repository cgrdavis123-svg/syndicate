Most delays come from waiting on content, so here’s everything we’ll ask for. Upload files in your [[projects-files-and-approvals|client portal]].

## Content

- **Your logo**, ideally as an SVG, PDF or large PNG. No logo? Add [[extra-pages-copywriting-logo|logo design]].
- **Photos** of your work, team, premises or products. Phone photos are fine if they’re sharp and well lit.
- **Text** for each page: what you do, where, prices if you show them, and how to reach you. Prefer not to write it? Add [[extra-pages-copywriting-logo|copywriting]].
- **Reviews** you’d like to show, with permission. We only ever use real reviews.

## Accounts

- **Your domain name login** (where you bought it), so we can connect it. See [[domains-and-hosting]].
- **Google Business Profile** access, if you have one. If not, we can set one up ({{price:add-gbp}}).

## Decisions

- Two or three websites you like the look of, and why.
- What you want visitors to do: call, book, fill in a form or buy.

> **Tip:** Large files like videos are easier to send as a Google Drive or Dropbox link. Paste it in a message in your portal.

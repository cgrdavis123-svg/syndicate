When you accept a [[custom-work-and-quotes|quote]], you pay a **deposit** online. Depending on the quote, that’s 100%, 50%, 25% or nothing up front.

- **Work starts** once the deposit is paid.
- **The balance** is invoiced when the work is delivered, due within 14 days.
- **Monthly items** in a quote start a monthly plan that renews until cancelled, with 30 days’ notice.
- **Ad spend** is always paid to the platforms directly.
- **The deposit is fully refundable** before the kickoff call; after that, refunds are based on work completed.
- Once paid in full, the work is yours. See [[who-owns-my-website]].

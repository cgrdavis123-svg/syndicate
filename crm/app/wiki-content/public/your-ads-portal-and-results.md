Your ads portal shows this month by default. Switch to last month or the last 90 days at the top.

## The four numbers

- **Ad spend:** what the platforms charged. The bar shows spend against your monthly budget; the small line marks where spending should be by today.
- **Leads:** people who came from your ads and then called, emailed, filled in a form or bought. When [[what-is-site-stats|Site Stats]] is on your website, we count these from your site; otherwise we use the platforms’ own numbers. The portal says which.
- **Cost per lead:** ad spend divided by leads. The single most useful number for judging ads.
- **Clicks:** people who clicked an ad. More clicks isn’t always better; clicks that turn into leads are what count.

## Campaigns

Each campaign shows its spend, clicks and conversions, and who sees it. “Waiting for you” means it needs your approval.

## How fresh are the numbers?

We update results from the platforms regularly; the chart shows the latest day included.

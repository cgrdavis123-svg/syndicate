Every new ad goes to you for approval first. Nothing goes live without your OK.

## How it works

1. We email you when ads are ready.
2. Open **Approvals** in {{adsportal}}. Each ad is shown the way people will see it: image or video, text, headline and button.
3. Press **Approve**, or **Ask for changes** and tell us what to change.
4. We make the changes and send it back, or get the approved ad live and mark it as running.

If you haven’t looked after two days, we send one friendly reminder.

> **Tip:** Specific feedback helps: “Use the photo of the van and say same-day service” beats “make it pop”.

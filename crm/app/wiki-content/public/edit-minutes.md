|---|---|---|
| {{name:care-plan}} | 1 hour | two business days |
| {{name:care-plus}} | 2 hours | one business day |

- We log the minutes each request takes. Your portal shows a meter of time used this month.
- Time resets on the 1st and **doesn’t roll over**.
- Monitoring, scans, updates, backups and your report don’t use edit time.
- If a request would take you past your monthly time, we tell you first and you choose: wait for next month, or have us quote it.

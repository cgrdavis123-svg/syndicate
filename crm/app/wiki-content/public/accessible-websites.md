Yes. Most visitors arrive on a phone, so we design for phones first and then larger screens.

We also follow accessibility basics so more people can use your site: readable text contrast, labels on every form field, descriptions on images, and pages that work with a keyboard. We aim for the WCAG AA contrast standard.

If you need a formal accessibility audit for legal or contract reasons, ask us and we’ll quote it.

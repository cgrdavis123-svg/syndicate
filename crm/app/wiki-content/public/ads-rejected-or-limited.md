---
title: What if a platform rejects my ad?
category: ads
order: 110
summary: It happens. We fix it and resubmit.
keywords: rejected, disapproved, policy, limited, ad review
---
Google, Meta and TikTok review every ad and sometimes reject or limit one under their own rules. Common reasons are certain words (like health claims or “you”), image text, or the landing page.

When that happens we fix the ad and resubmit, and tell you if it changes anything you approved. Some industries (finance, health, housing, alcohol) have extra rules; we’ll flag anything that affects you at kickoff.

We can’t override a platform’s decision, but we can appeal ones we think are wrong.

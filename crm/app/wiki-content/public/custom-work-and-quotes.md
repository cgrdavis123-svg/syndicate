Our packages cover most small businesses, but not everything. If you need something different, like a bigger website, a booking system, or a mix of services, we’ll quote it.

## How quotes work

1. Tell us what you need on a [[book-a-discovery-call|discovery call]] or by email at {{email}}.
2. We send you a quote by email. It opens as a web page with every item and its price.
3. Some items may be **optional**: tick the ones you want and the total updates.
4. **Accept online** by typing your name, and pay the deposit by card.

Quotes are valid for **30 days**, and we send one reminder three days before one expires. See [[quotes-and-deposits]] for how deposits and the final balance work.

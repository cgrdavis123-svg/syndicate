Every booking confirmation email has a link to **reschedule or cancel** yourself, and a calendar invite.

- Pick a new time any time up to the start of the call.
- Paid consultations can be rescheduled. To cancel one and get a refund, email {{email}} before the call.

Lost the email? Email us and we’ll move it for you. You’ll also get a reminder the day before your call.

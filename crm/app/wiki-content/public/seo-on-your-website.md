Every website we build includes the on-page basics that help search engines understand it:

- Page titles and descriptions written for each page.
- A sitemap, so search engines find every page.
- Fast loading and a mobile-friendly layout, which Google rewards.
- Clear headings and image descriptions.

The **{{name:web-starter}}** adds on-page SEO for your services and a link to your Google Business Profile. The **{{name:web-business}}** adds full on-page SEO and speed tuning.

## What we can’t promise

No honest agency can guarantee a ranking or a first-page spot. Rankings depend on your competition, your reviews and how long your site has been around. What we can do is make sure nothing on your website holds you back.

> **Tip:** For local businesses, a complete Google Business Profile with real reviews often matters as much as the website itself.

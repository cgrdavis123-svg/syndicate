You keep ownership of your ad accounts, and the ad spend is billed to you. Adding us as a user or partner lets us run the ads, and you can remove our access any time.

Your **Setup** page in {{adsportal}} has the exact steps for your accounts, including the IDs to enter. In general:

## Google Ads

Send us your 10-digit Customer ID (top right in Google Ads), then accept our link request under **Admin → Access and security → Managers**. Or invite our email as a user with Standard access.

## Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

In **Meta Business Settings**, add us as a **partner** (or a person) with access to your **ad account**, **Facebook Page** and **pixel (dataset)**.

## TikTok

In **TikTok Business Center**, add us as a **partner** and share your ad account and pixel.

## Don’t have accounts yet?

We can create them for you, in your business’s name, with **{{name:add-ads-setup}}** ({{price:add-ads-setup}}). See [[ad-account-setup]].

> **Tip:** The platforms change their menus often. If something doesn’t match, message us from your ads portal and we’ll walk you through it on a quick call.

title: How are monthly plans billed?
category: billing

---
Ads management and care plans are billed **monthly, starting the day you order**, and renew automatically on the same date each month until cancelled.

- See all your plans, their next billing date and how to cancel in {{plans}}.
- We email you once a year with each plan’s price, how often it bills, and how to cancel.
- We give you **at least 30 days’ notice** by email before any price change.
- **Ad spend** is never part of these charges; the ad platforms bill you for it directly. See [[ad-spend-vs-our-fee]].

If a payment fails, see [[failed-payments]].

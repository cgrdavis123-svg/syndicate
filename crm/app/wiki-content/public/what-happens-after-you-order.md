Here’s what happens the moment your payment goes through.

## Straight away

- **A receipt and order confirmation** arrive by email from {{email}}.
- **Your client portal opens.** We email you a sign-in link. Your project is already there, with each step listed. See [[signing-in-to-your-portal]].
- If your order includes a website or ads, your **[[what-is-site-stats|Site Stats]]** dashboard is set up for you.

## Within one business day

We email you to book your **[[the-kickoff-call|kickoff call]]**. The confirmation email also has a link so you can book it yourself.

## Monthly plans

If you bought an ads plan or a care plan, it renews each month on the same date. You can see and cancel your plans any time in {{plans}}. See [[monthly-plans-billing]].

> **Note:** If you paid by bank transfer, your order shows as “processing” until the payment clears, usually within a few business days. We start once it does.

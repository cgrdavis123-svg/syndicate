## How to choose

- **{{name:web-one-page}} ({{price:web-one-page}}):** you need a professional presence fast, and your customers mostly want to call or message you.
- **{{name:web-starter}} ({{price:web-starter}}):** you want separate pages for your services, an about page and a contact page, and to show up in local search.
- **{{name:web-business}} ({{price:web-business}}):** you have more to say, want a blog, want to edit text yourself, or plan to run ads to a dedicated landing page.
- **{{name:web-store}} ({{price:web-store}}):** you sell products online and need checkout, shipping and tax.

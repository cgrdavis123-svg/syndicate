## A–C

- **Ad spend**: what you pay Google, Meta or TikTok for your ads to run. Separate from our fee.
- **Bounce**: a visit that saw one page and left.
- **Campaign**: a group of ads with one goal, budget and audience.
- **Conversion**: an action you care about, like a call, form or purchase. Also called a **goal** or **lead**.
- **Cost per click (CPC)**: ad spend divided by clicks.
- **Cost per lead (CPL)**: ad spend divided by leads. The best single measure of whether ads are paying off.
- **Core Web Vitals**: Google’s three measures of how fast a page feels. See [[speed-numbers-explained]].
- **CTR (click-through rate)**: the share of people who saw an ad and clicked it.

## D–P

- **DNS**: the settings that point your domain at your website and email.
- **Domain**: your web address, like yourbusiness.com. See [[domains-and-hosting]].
- **Hosting**: the server your website lives on.
- **Impressions**: how many times your ads were shown.
- **Landing page**: the page an ad sends people to.
- **PageSpeed**: Google’s lab test of a page’s speed and quality. See [[pagespeed-scores-explained]].
- **Pixel (or tag)**: a snippet from an ad platform that lets it count conversions from your ads.

## R–Z

- **Retargeting**: showing ads to people who already visited your site.
- **ROAS**: return on ad spend, sales divided by ad spend. Mostly used for online stores.
- **SSL certificate**: what gives your site the padlock and https.
- **Uptime**: the share of time your website was up and working.
- **UTM tags**: labels added to a link (like `utm_campaign=spring-sale`) so you can see which ad or email a visit came from. See [[where-visitors-come-from]].

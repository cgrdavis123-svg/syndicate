Creator partners are people who post videos, stream or run an audience of business owners, Discord owners or sellers. They get their own easy code instead of a plain referral link.

## If you have a creator’s code

Type it in the **Creator code** box on the last step of checkout, or open the creator’s link, which fills it in for you.

- It takes **10% off the one-time items** in your first order: website packages, add-ons, audits and Discord setup. Monthly plans are full price.
- It’s for someone new to us. If you’ve already paid us, or you’re already talking to us about a project, checkout will say the code doesn’t apply.
- It doesn’t combine with a special offer or a promo code.

The creator you used it for sees your business name and whether you became a client. They don’t see what you bought or your contact details.

## If you’re a creator

Join on the [creator page](https://syndicatemarketing.co/creators.html#join). Pick a code of 3 to 20 letters and numbers, like your handle.

- You earn **10%** of what each client you bring in pays us in their first **12 months**, after the code’s discount. Ad spend and sales tax don’t count.
- Each earning is held 30 days in case of a refund, then paid monthly as cash (from $25, W-9 needed) or account credit.
- There’s no follower minimum and no posting quota.

> **Heads up:** every mention of us has to be labelled as an ad. Turn on the platform’s paid-partnership or branded-content label and say it in your own words, for example “#ad — I earn a commission if you use my code.” Mentions without a clear label don’t earn.

Everything else works like the [[referral-program]]. Sign in to [your partner page](https://crm.stusyn.com/refer.php) to see your code, your referrals and what you’ve earned.

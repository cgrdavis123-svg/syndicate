- **Receipts** from Stripe arrive by email when you pay.
- **Invoices** from us (numbered like SYN-1001) are emailed and kept in {{portal}}. Each one opens as a page you can print or save as a PDF.
- **Monthly plans** create a paid invoice each month, so you have a record for your bookkeeping.

Unpaid invoices are due within **14 days** unless the invoice says otherwise, and include a link to pay online.

Need an invoice with different details, like a company name or tax number? Email {{email}}.

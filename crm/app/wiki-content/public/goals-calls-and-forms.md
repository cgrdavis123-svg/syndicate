A **goal** is an action you care about. Site Stats counts these automatically:

- **Phone call**: someone taps your phone number.
- **Email click**: someone taps your email address.
- **Form sent**: someone submits a form.

On online stores and our own checkout we also count purchases and their value.

## Custom goals

We can add others, like “Booked an appointment” or “Downloaded the menu”, either when a certain page is visited (like a thank-you page) or when a button is pressed. Ask us and we’ll set it up.

> **Good to know:** A tap on your phone number counts as a call attempt. Whether the call connected happens outside the website, so treat it as a strong signal rather than an exact count.

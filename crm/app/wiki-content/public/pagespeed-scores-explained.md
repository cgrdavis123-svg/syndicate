Each week we run Google’s **PageSpeed** test on your home page, as a visitor on a phone would see it. It gives four scores out of 100:

| Score | What it measures | Good |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | How quickly the page loads and becomes usable | 90+ |
| **Accessibility** | Whether people with disabilities can use it | 90+ |
| **Best practices** | Security and modern web standards | 90+ |
| **Search (SEO)** | Whether search engines can read it properly | 90+ |

50–89 needs work; under 50 is slow. The small ▲▼ shows the change since last week.

## Why scores move

Scores vary a little from test to test, even with no changes, because of network conditions. Look at the trend, not one result. Big drops usually mean a new large image, a new plugin or a slow third-party script.

---
title: What happens if my website goes down?
category: care
order: 30
summary: We’re alerted within about half an hour, you get an email, and we fix it.
keywords: down, outage, offline, not loading, site down, error
---
If you’re on a care plan:

1. Our monitor checks your site every 15 minutes. **Two failed checks in a row** counts as down, so a one-second blip doesn’t raise an alarm.
2. **We get an alert** straight away, and so do you (you can ask us to turn your emails off).
3. We find the cause and fix it. If the problem is with your hosting company, we deal with them for you where we can.
4. When it’s back, **you get another email** saying how long it was down.

Every outage is listed on your Website care page and in your monthly report.

> **Heads up:** Not on a care plan? Email {{email}} and we’ll help, but we won’t know about it until you tell us. See [[care-plans-compared]].

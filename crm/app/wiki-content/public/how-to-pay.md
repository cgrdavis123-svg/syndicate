title: How do I pay?
category: billing

---
You pay on **Stripe’s secure checkout page** by card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), Apple Pay or Google Pay. Some customers also see a bank payment option. We never see or store your card number.

- **One-time packages and services** are paid in full when you order.
- **Monthly plans** (ads and care) are charged when you order and then on the same date each month. See [[monthly-plans-billing]].
- **Custom quotes** are paid as a deposit when you accept, with the balance invoiced on delivery. See [[quotes-and-deposits]].

You get a receipt by email straight away. See [[invoices-and-receipts]].

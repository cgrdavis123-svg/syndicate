| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| **Before your kickoff** (your kickoff call, or sending your kickoff form if you skip the call) | Full refund |
| **After work has started** | At our discretion, based on the work not yet done |
| **Monthly plans** | Months that have started aren’t refunded; see [[cancel-a-plan]] |
| **Audits and strategy sessions** | Full refund until the work is delivered or the call takes place |
| **Quote deposits** | Full refund before the kickoff call; after that, based on work completed |

To ask for a refund, email {{email}} with your order number. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.

The full wording is in our {{terms}}.

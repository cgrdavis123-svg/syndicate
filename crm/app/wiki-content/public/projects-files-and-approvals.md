Each project in your portal shows:

- **Steps**: the checklist from kickoff to launch, ticked off as we go, with a progress bar.
- **Updates**: news, drafts and questions from us. Reply right there.
- **Files**: upload your logo, photos and text, and download anything we share.

## Sending files

Drag files onto the project page or choose them. The page shows the size limit. For large files like videos, paste a Google Drive or Dropbox link in a message instead.

## Approving work

When a draft is ready, we post it as an update. Reply with your changes, or say it’s good to go. See [[rounds-of-changes]].

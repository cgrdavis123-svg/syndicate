Early each month we email you a report on the month before. It opens as a web page with charts, and you can print it or save it as a PDF.

It covers:

- **Uptime**: one bar per day, any outages and how long they lasted, and average response time.
- **Speed**: your PageSpeed scores and the biggest speed wins left, plus what real visitors experienced.
- **Security and health**: the weekly scan in plain words, and your certificate and domain renewal dates.
- **Visitors**: from Site Stats, once it’s on your site.
- **Work we did**: every update, backup, fix and edit, and the edit time used.

All your reports are on the Website care page in {{portal}}.

Site Stats measures how fast your site feels to **real visitors**, using Google’s Core Web Vitals. We show the experience of 3 in 4 visitors (the 75th percentile), the same way Google judges sites.

| Number | Plain meaning | Good |
|---|---|---|
| **Main content shows** (LCP) | How long until the main thing on the page appears | under 2.5 s |
| **Reacts to taps** (INP) | How quickly the page responds when someone taps or clicks | under 200 ms |
| **Layout stays put** (CLS) | How much things jump around while loading | under 0.10 |

These come from real phones and computers, so they’re different from the lab test in [[pagespeed-scores-explained|PageSpeed scores]]. Both matter; real-visitor numbers are what your customers actually feel.

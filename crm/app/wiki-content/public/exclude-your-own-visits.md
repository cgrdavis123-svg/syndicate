---
title: How do I stop my own visits being counted?
category: analytics
order: 50
summary: Visit your site once with ?syn_ignore=1 on the end.
keywords: ignore, exclude, my visits, own traffic, filter
---
You probably visit your own website a lot. To keep your numbers about customers:

1. Open your website in each browser you use (phone and computer).
2. Add `?syn_ignore=1` to the end of the address, like `https://yourbusiness.com/?syn_ignore=1`, and press enter.

That browser is now ignored. Do it again if you clear your browser data or get a new device.

To be counted again, visit with `?syn_ignore=0`.

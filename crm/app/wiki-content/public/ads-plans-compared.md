## How to choose

- **One platform:** start where your customers already are. For local services, that’s usually **Google** (people searching right now). For products and visual businesses, **Meta** (Facebook and Instagram) or **TikTok**.
- **Two platforms:** reach people on search and on social, with retargeting between them.
- **All three:** for businesses ready to test everything; includes short video creative.

Every plan includes setup, new creative each month, conversion tracking, results in your ads portal, a monthly report and a check-in call. Cancel with 30 days’ notice (see [[changing-or-cancelling-ads]]).

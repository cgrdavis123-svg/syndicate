1. Sign in to {{plans}}.
2. Press **Cancel** next to the plan.
3. You’ll see the exact date it ends. Confirm, and we email you a confirmation.

You can also email {{email}} or reply to any receipt, and we’ll cancel it the same business day.

## When it ends

| Plan | Ends |
|---|---|
| **Care plans** | When the month you’ve already paid for runs out |
| **Ads management** | On the first billing date at least **30 days** after you cancel, so you may be billed once more inside those 30 days |

Months that have started aren’t refunded. When an ads plan ends, we pause your campaigns and remove our access; your accounts and data stay yours.

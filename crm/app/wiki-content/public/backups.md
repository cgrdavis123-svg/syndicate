---
title: How are backups handled?
category: care
order: 60
summary: Weekly backups on most sites, monthly content exports on hosted platforms.
keywords: backup, restore, recovery, lost, hacked
---
- **WordPress and hand-built sites:** we take a full backup **every week** and log it, so you can see each one on your Website care page.
- **Shopify, Squarespace, Wix and Webflow:** these platforms back up their servers themselves. We export your content **every month** instead.

Need something restored? Request it from your portal like any other change.

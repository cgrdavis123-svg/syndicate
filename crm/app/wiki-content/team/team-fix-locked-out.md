---
title: A client can’t sign in
category: team-fixes
order: 50
summary: Portal and ads portal sign-in problems.
---
- Sign-in links expire in 30 minutes and work once. Ask them to request a new one.
- Check the email on their contact record, and that **portal access** is on.
- They may be using a different email from the one they ordered with.
- Ads portal: their company must have an ads account; otherwise they see “No ads plan on this account”.
- Still stuck: send their invite link from the contact page in the CRM.

---
title: How we write
category: team-rules
order: 40
summary: Plain, direct, warm and honest.
---
- Write like a capable person talking to a small-business owner. Short sentences. No jargon unless explained.
- Say what’s included, what it costs and how long it takes.
- Never: “synergy”, “revolutionary”, “world-class”, guaranteed results, made-up stats, fake urgency or scarcity.
- “We” for the brands; “Grady” in the founder story.
- Curly quotes and apostrophes, en dashes for ranges (4–5 weeks).
- Specific button words: “Book a free discovery call”, “Add to cart”, “Approve”.
- Every customer email comes from hello@stusyn.com (careers from careers@), signed by the brand, with a clear next step.
- Help center titles are the question a client would ask.

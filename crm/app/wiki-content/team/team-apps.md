| Address | What | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| stusyn.com | Parent company site, careers, Hiring HQ (/admin) | Public, staff |
| syndicatemarketing.co | Agency site, store and checkout | Public |
| crm.stusyn.com | CRM, client portal, booking, quotes, invoices, Site Stats, care | Staff, clients |
| ads.stusyn.com | Ads management and the client ads portal | Staff, ads clients |
| wiki.stusyn.com | Help center and team docs | Public, staff |

Staff sign in to the CRM, ads and wiki with the same account and two-step sign-in. Clients sign in to the portal and ads portal with emailed links. The ads app and wiki run from the CRM’s `ads/` and `wiki/` folders on the same database.

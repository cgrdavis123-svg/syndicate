---
title: Answering help center questions
category: team-clients
order: 60
summary: Turn every question into a better help center.
---
1. Questions from the **Ask us** form arrive by email and as tasks, and wait in **Wiki → Questions & insights**.
2. Reply by email within one business day.
3. If others are likely to ask the same thing, press **Write an article about it**. Write the title as the question people ask.
4. Mark the question **answered**.

Also check **Searches with no answer** and **Articles that didn’t help** each week. Use `{{price:sku}}` and other tags instead of typing prices.

These appear on the sites, in the catalog, the terms of sale, quote terms and the help center. If one changes, update all of them.

- Reply within **one business day**.
- Rounds of changes: **2** (one-page, starter), **3** (business, store).
- Timelines: one-page ~2 weeks, starter ~3, business ~4–5, store ~6; audits 7 days; analytics setup 5 days; first ads ~2–3 weeks.
- Refunds: full before kickoff; after that based on work not done; monthly months started aren’t refunded.
- Cancellation online; ads end at the first billing date 30+ days after cancelling; care ends when the paid month runs out.
- Care requests started within **two business days** (care) or **one** (Care Plus); edit time doesn’t roll over; we warn before going over the allowance.
- Strategy session credited within 30 days.
- 30 days’ notice before monthly price changes.
- Discovery calls Mon–Thu 9–5, Fri 9–3 Eastern.

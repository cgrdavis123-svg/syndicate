title: Writing a quote
category: team-clients
order: 50
summary: Custom work that doesn’t fit a package.
---
1. CRM → **Quotes** → New. Pick the brand and client.
2. Add items from the catalog or custom lines. One-off or monthly. Mark nice-to-haves as **optional** so the client can tick them.
3. Choose the deposit: 100%, 50%, 25% or 0%.
4. Check the default terms, then **Send**. The client gets a link to view, choose options, accept and pay.

On a win, the CRM creates the deal, paid deposit invoice, draft balance invoice, project, portal access, Site Stats site, ads client or care plan where relevant, and the kickoff task. Send the balance invoice on delivery (due in 14 days).

Quotes expire after 30 days; one reminder goes out 3 days before.

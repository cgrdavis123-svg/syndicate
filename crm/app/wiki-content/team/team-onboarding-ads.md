---
title: Onboarding an ads client
category: team-clients
order: 20
summary: Access, tracking, plan, approval, launch.
---
The ads client is created automatically from the order. On their page at ads.stusyn.com:

1. **Kickoff call**: goals, area, budget, what a customer is worth, offers. Tick **Kickoff call** in the setup checklist. Set the **monthly budget** and **target cost per lead** under Budget & goals.
2. **Access**: the client follows their Setup page. When access arrives, set each account to **Access granted** and add the account name and ID. If they bought ad account setup, create accounts in their business’s name.
3. **Tracking**: install pixels and conversion tracking, test a form and a call tap, then tick **Pixel** and **Conversions verified**.
4. **Plan**: create campaigns (status Draft) with audience text the client will see, and a **UTM campaign** so Site Stats can match leads.
5. **Ads**: create creatives, send for approval together. Nothing goes live until approved.
6. **Launch**: build in the platform, set campaigns to **Live** (logged automatically), mark approved ads **Live**.
7. Import results within the first week to confirm everything flows.

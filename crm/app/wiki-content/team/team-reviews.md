## What happens

A few days after a project is marked **Complete** (3 by default), the client gets one email from hello@ asking for an honest Google review, with a link straight to our Google Business Profile. The CRM logs it on the contact and the company, and shows it on the project page and in **Settings → Review requests**, including whether they opened the link.

Each business is asked **at most once a year**, however many projects they finish. Projects that were already finished when this was switched on were never asked.

## The rules (don’t bend these)

- **Everyone gets the same email and the same link.** Never check whether someone is happy first and only send the link to happy clients. That’s “review gating”, and Google removes reviews and can suspend the profile for it.
- **Nothing in return.** No discount, credit, gift or entry into a draw for leaving a review. The FTC’s rule on consumer reviews (2024) bans buying reviews, and Google does too.
- **No suggested wording or star rating**, and never write a review for a client or post one from our side.
- Don’t ask friends, family or anyone who hasn’t paid us for work.

## When it doesn’t send

The request waits or is skipped when: an invoice is overdue (it waits; after a month it’s skipped), they had a refund in the last six months, they’re on the outreach do-not-contact list, the business was already asked this year, the project is hidden from the client portal, it isn’t a Syndicate Marketing project, or there’s no email address. Each skipped row says why.

From the project page or Review requests you can **Send now** (skips the wait, not the rules) or **Don’t ask**. A skipped request can be asked after all.

## Setup (once)

In Google Business Profile, choose **Ask for reviews** and copy the link (it looks like `https://g.page/r/…/review`). Paste it in **Settings → Review requests**. Until it’s there, requests wait and nothing goes out.

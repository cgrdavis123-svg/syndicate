---
title: Onboarding a website client
category: team-clients
order: 10
summary: From kickoff to launch.
---
1. **Kickoff.** Most clients fill in the online kickoff form, which starts the draft by itself. If they book a call instead: goals, customers, style, pages, deadline; agree dates; confirm domain and hosting (in the client’s name, always). Straight after, write what you learned in **Website drafts → the client → Notes for the builder**, and the contact details they approved for the site.
2. **Collect content** in the portal: logo, photos, text, reviews they’ve given permission to use. Offer copywriting or a logo if they’re stuck.
3. **Build.** The builder agent drafts it from the brief, their files and your notes, following `templates/CLAUDE.md` ([[team-website-drafts]]). You can also build by hand: press **Hold** on the draft and work as before.
4. **Drafts** go to the client for approval from the draft page, after you’ve looked them over. The CRM counts rounds (2 for one-page and starter, 3 for business and store) and asks you when they run out.
5. **Launch**: connect the domain, check forms reach the client, add Site Stats goals, visit with `?syn_ignore=1`, test on a phone.
6. **Hand over**: show the Site Stats dashboard; for business/store sites, show how to edit. Offer a care plan if they don’t have one.
7. Mark the project **Complete**.

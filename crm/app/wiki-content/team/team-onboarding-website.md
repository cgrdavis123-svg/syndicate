---
title: Onboarding a website client
category: team-clients
order: 10
summary: From kickoff to launch.
---
1. **Kickoff call.** Goals, customers, style, pages, deadline. Agree dates. Confirm domain and hosting (in the client’s name, always).
2. **Collect content** in the portal: logo, photos, text, reviews they’ve given permission to use. Offer copywriting or a logo if they’re stuck.
3. **Build** following `templates/CLAUDE.md` (stack, design, accessibility, Site Stats, forms, testing).
4. **Drafts** go to the client as portal updates. Track rounds (2 for one-page and starter, 3 for business and store).
5. **Launch**: connect the domain, check forms reach the client, add Site Stats goals, visit with `?syn_ignore=1`, test on a phone.
6. **Hand over**: show the Site Stats dashboard; for business/store sites, show how to edit. Offer a care plan if they don’t have one.
7. Mark the project **Complete**.

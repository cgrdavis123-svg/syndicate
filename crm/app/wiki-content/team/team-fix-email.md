---
title: Emails aren’t arriving
category: team-fixes
order: 20
summary: When clients say they didn’t get our emails.
---
1. Ask them to check spam and promotions for hello@stusyn.com.
2. Check the address on their contact record for typos.
3. Make sure SPF, DKIM and DMARC are set for stusyn.com (cPanel → Email Deliverability). Missing records are the most common cause of spam filtering.
4. For portal sign-in links: they expire in 30 minutes and work once. Send a fresh one, or use the contact’s invite link from the CRM.

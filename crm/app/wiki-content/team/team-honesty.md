---
title: Honesty rules
category: team-rules
order: 10
summary: What we never publish or promise.
---
- **No fabricated** reviews, testimonials, clients, logos, case studies, stats or results. Ever.
- Portfolio concepts are **labelled fictional**, use `.example` domains and 555-01xx numbers, and are noindex.
- Only name real companies as clients if they are (Intake Breathing and Rap Snacks may be named).
- **No guarantees**: no guaranteed rankings, ROAS, leads or “#1”.
- Example figures are always labelled as examples.
- Only use real reviews, with permission. When asking for reviews, ask for honest ones and never offer anything in return.

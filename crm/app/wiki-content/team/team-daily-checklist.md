summary: What to look at every working day, in order.
---
1. **CRM dashboard** (crm.stusyn.com): tasks due today, new deals, unpaid invoices, store orders, bookings.
2. **Inbox**: reply to anything from clients within one business day.
3. **Care** (crm.stusyn.com/care.php): anything down, open edit requests, backups due.
4. **Ads** (ads.stusyn.com): the “Needs attention” list. Messages and change requests first, then pacing, then stale results.
5. **Wiki → Questions & insights**: answer help center questions by email, then mark them answered.
6. **Pipeline**: move deals forward; follow up on quotes and unfinished checkouts.

> **Tip:** Every client email, request and form creates a task. If the task list is empty, you’re caught up.

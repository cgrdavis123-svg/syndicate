## Weekly

- **Import results** for every platform (ads.stusyn.com → Import results). Use a daily breakdown; re-importing a day replaces it. Overlap the last import by a few days.
- **Check pacing** on the overview. Over 115% or under 75% of pace means adjust budgets.
- **Log what you change** in the client’s work log (shared entries appear in their portal and report): budget moves, paused keywords, new audiences, tracking fixes.
- **New creative**: aim for fresh ads every month; send for approval in batches.

## Monthly

Write the report notes (see [[team-monthly-routine]]) and hold the check-in call.

## Leads

“Leads” come from Site Stats (paid-channel visits that hit a goal) when the client’s site has it; otherwise from platform conversions. If they disagree a lot, check tracking.

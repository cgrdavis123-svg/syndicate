## Days 1–5

- **Ads report notes.** On the 1st you get a task per ads client: open their page on ads.stusyn.com → **Monthly report**, write “How the month went” and “Next month”, and save. The report emails once notes are saved, or on day 5 regardless. Import the last few days of results first.
- **Care reports** send themselves (days 1–5). Glance at each one: anything odd in uptime or the scan?
- **Site Stats reports** send themselves for non-care sites.

## Also monthly

- **Care Plus**: quarterly tune-up tasks appear in the first week of each quarter; yearly review-call tasks near each anniversary.
- **Backups**: clear any “Back up …” tasks.
- **Recurring invoices** go out by themselves; check none bounced.

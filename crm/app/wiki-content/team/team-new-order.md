When Stripe confirms payment, the CRM automatically:

- creates or updates the **contact and company**, and a **Won deal** (source “Marketing site — checkout”);
- records a **paid invoice**;
- creates the **project** with steps from each product;
- turns on **client portal** access and emails a sign-in link;
- creates a **Site Stats site** if a product needs one;
- for a **care plan**: turns on monitoring (and onboarding tasks if we didn’t build the site);
- for an **ads plan**: creates the ads client on ads.stusyn.com with platforms, budget and area from checkout, plus onboarding tasks;
- emails the customer a confirmation with the kickoff booking link, and emails us an alert.

## Your part

1. Check the order in **Store** and read the brief.
2. Clear the **“Schedule kickoff call”** task; most clients book it themselves from the email.
3. Work through the onboarding tasks.

Unpaid checkouts get one reminder email 1–48 hours later, and become a follow-up deal after a day.

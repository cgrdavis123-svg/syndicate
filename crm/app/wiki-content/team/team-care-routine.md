---
title: Looking after care plan sites
category: team-clients
order: 40
summary: Backups, requests, logging and alerts.
---
- **Edit requests** arrive by email and as tasks. Open the site in **Care**, set the request to **In progress**, do the work, then set **Done** with the minutes and a short note (the client is emailed). Minutes count toward their monthly allowance.
- **Backups**: take them on schedule (weekly; monthly content exports for Shopify, Squarespace, Wix and Webflow) and press **Log a backup**. Overdue backups create tasks.
- **Updates and fixes**: log them as shared entries so the client sees them in the portal and report.
- **Alerts**: down/up emails, SSL and domain warnings, exposed files and broken links all create tasks. Exposed files are urgent.
- **Over the allowance?** Tell the client before starting, and offer to wait for next month or quote it.
- **Care Plus**: quarterly speed and search tune-up (start from the PageSpeed fixes, log as Speed) and a yearly review call.

1. Our commitment

Syndicate Holdings Group is working to make stusyn.com accessible to people of all abilities. Our goal is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

2. What we do

  • Every page and form can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Form fields have labels, and errors are explained in text, not just with color.
  • Text and controls are designed for strong contrast against the dark background.
  • Animations are turned off for anyone who has “reduce motion” set on their device.
  • Pages use proper headings and landmarks so screen readers can navigate them.
  • Decorative animations and graphics are hidden from assistive technology.
  • The site works at phone sizes and when zoomed to 200%.

3. Known limitations

Some decorative animated backgrounds may be distracting for some visitors. You can turn them off with your device’s reduce-motion setting. We review the site regularly, and we fix issues as we find them.

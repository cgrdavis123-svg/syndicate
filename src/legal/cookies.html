1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, like your browser’s local storage, work in much the same way. Sites use them to remember things between page loads, such as keeping a form secure or remembering a setting.

Strictly necessary cookies don’t require consent, because the site can’t work properly without them. They aren’t used for advertising or to track you on other sites.

3. Optional cookies

Analytics: we count visits with our own analytics, which sets no cookies and saves nothing on your device. It’s described in our Privacy Policy. It runs unless you turn Analytics off in Cookie settings (or choose “Turn all off”), and you can change that at any time.

Marketing: we don’t use any marketing cookies or ad pixels. If we ever add one, it will stay off by default and only turn on if you choose, and we’ll list it here first.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, optional cookies stay off and can’t be turned on from our settings.

4. Third parties

No third-party cookies are set through stusyn.com. Our fonts and scripts are hosted on our own server, except Cloudflare Turnstile, the bot check on our contact and job application forms. It loads from Cloudflare in its own frame and is used only for that check, not for advertising or tracking. If you follow a link to another site, like TikTok, LinkedIn or a brand partner, that site’s own cookie policy applies.

5. How to control cookies

  • Use on this site.
  • Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies. If you block strictly necessary cookies, job application forms may not work.
  • Turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser or with an extension.

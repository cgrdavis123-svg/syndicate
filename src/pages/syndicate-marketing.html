How much does a website cost?

It depends on the size of the site and what it needs to do, like online booking or ordering. Every website is quoted as a fixed project price after a short call, so you know the cost before we start. Care plans for hosting and updates are a monthly fee.

How do you charge for ads?

We charge a management fee for planning, creative and running the campaigns. Your ad spend goes straight to Google, Meta or TikTok from your own account, so you always see exactly what was spent and keep the account if we ever part ways.

Do I have to use your analytics?

It comes with every site and campaign we run, and it works alongside Google Analytics and the Meta and Google ad tags if you already use them. It’s the dashboard we use to manage your campaigns, so you see exactly what we see.

Can you work on a website someone else built?

Yes. We can improve, speed up or redesign an existing WordPress, Shopify, Squarespace, Wix or Webflow site, add our stats to it, and run ads to it. If a rebuild would serve you better, we’ll tell you why.

Do you only work with businesses in Spartanburg?

No. We’re based in Spartanburg and know the Upstate well, but we work remotely with businesses anywhere in the US.

Questions

Before you order.

Want something bigger? Ask for a custom website quote.

Who is a one-page website right for?

Local service businesses, new businesses, single offers and events: anyone who needs a professional site that gets calls and quote requests, without paying for pages nobody reads. If you need a store, many pages or logins, we’ll quote a custom build instead.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo (or we’ll use a clean text logo), a few photos, your services, hours, service area and contact details. We’ll write the page from what you send and a short kickoff call.

Is hosting included?

The {{onePagePrice}} covers design and build. You’ll need a domain name and hosting in your own name, which usually costs a few dollars a month. We’ll help you pick and set them up, or you can ask about a care plan where we handle hosting and updates.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once it’s paid for and launched, the site, the text we wrote for it and the domain are yours.

Can I add ads or more pages later?

Yes. A one-page site is a great landing page for Google, Meta and TikTok ads, and we can add pages any time. Because your stats are already running, you’ll see exactly what the ads bring in.

Signs your website is costing you customers

  • It looks off on a phone.Most people will see your site on their phone first. Tiny text, slow pages or buttons that are hard to tap send them back to Google.
  • People can’t call you in one tap.Your number, hours and address should be right there, not buried on a contact page.
  • You can’t change anything yourself.New prices, a new service, holiday hours: every update means waiting on someone.
  • It doesn’t show up in searches.Without titles, descriptions and pages for each service, Google has little to go on.
  • You don’t know if it works.How many people visited? How many called or filled in the form? Most owners have no idea.

What’s the catch?

Why is it so much cheaper than your normal price?

It’s a limited offer for a small number of new clients. The work is exactly the same Starter website package that normally sells for {{offer:starter-500:list.web-starter}}. To keep the quality right, there are only {{offer:starter-500:spots}} spots, and the offer closes on {{offer:starter-500:ends}} or when they’re gone.

Are the spots and the deadline real?

Yes. The counter on this page comes straight from our order system, and checkout stops taking the offer once the spots are gone or the date passes. We won’t extend it or “find” extra spots.

What happens after the 3 free months?

Care Plus continues at {{offer:starter-500:monthly}}/month, charged automatically on the same date each month to the card you used. We email you a week before the first charge. If you don’t want it, cancel online before then in your client portal (Plans & billing) and you won’t pay anything more. You can also switch to the lighter Website care plan at {{price:care-plan}}.

How do I cancel Care Plus?

Sign in to your client portal with your email (no password), open Plans & billing and click Cancel. It takes two clicks and shows you the exact end date before you confirm. You can also just reply to any of our emails and we’ll cancel it for you.

Is the website mine?

Yes. Once it’s paid for, the site, the words and the design are yours, and your domain and hosting are in your name. If you ever leave, you take it with you.

Do I have to pay for a domain and hosting?

Yes, those are separate and stay in your name, so you always control them. A .com usually costs about $10–20 a year, paid to the registrar. We recommend reliable hosting and set it up with you at kickoff. Already have both? Great, we’ll use them.

I already have a website. Can you replace it?

Yes. We rebuild it as a modern site, keep the content worth keeping, point your existing domain at the new site and redirect old page addresses so you don’t lose visitors from Google.

How long does it take?

About 3 weeks from your kickoff call. Most of the waiting is usually on content (photos and text), so sending those early speeds things up. Need it sooner? Add rush delivery ({{price:add-rush}}) and it launches in about half the time.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo, some photos of your work or business, and the basics: what you do, where you work and how people reach you. If you don’t have a logo or don’t want to write the words, add logo design or copywriting, and we take care of it.

Can I make changes myself after launch?

The Starter website is built for us to keep up to date, so you don’t have to learn anything. With Care Plus you just ask in your portal: up to 2 hours of edits a month are included, started within one business day. If you’d rather edit it yourself, that comes with our Business website ({{price:web-business}}).

What if I need more than 5 pages?

Add extra pages at {{price:add-page}} each, like a page for every town you serve. For bigger custom features, we’ll send you a quote.

Will this get me to the top of Google?

We set up the basics Google needs to understand your site: proper titles and descriptions, fast pages, a sitemap and a link to your Google Business Profile. That gives you the best shot. Anyone who guarantees rankings isn’t being straight with you.

Can you run ads to it too?

Yes. Once your site is live, you can add ads management on Google, Meta or TikTok from {{price:ads-one}}. Ad spend is paid directly to the platforms, never through us.

How do I pay? Can I get a refund?

Pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay on Stripe’s secure checkout. If you change your mind before your kickoff call, you get a full refund. After that, refunds are based on the work done.

Can I talk to someone first?

Of course. Book a free 20-minute call, or email {{email}}. We reply within one business day. Booking a call doesn’t hold a spot, so if you’re sure, claim it first; you’re covered by the full refund before kickoff anyway.

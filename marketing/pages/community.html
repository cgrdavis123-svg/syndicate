Questions

Community FAQ

Do we have to already have a Discord server?

No. We can build one from scratch with Discord server setup and hand ownership to you, or take over an existing server. If yours has grown messy, a Community audit is a good first step.

How do you get access to our server?

You add our account and give it a staff role with the permissions we need, usually managing channels, roles and messages. We don’t need to be the owner, and you can remove us any time. Turning on two-factor authentication for staff is part of our setup.

Are you online all the time?

No, and we won’t pretend to be. Community management includes moderation check-ins every weekday; Plus adds weekends. Between check-ins, AutoMod and moderation bots catch spam, scam links and raids. With Plus we also recruit and train volunteer moderators from your community for extra coverage.

What happens if something serious comes up?

Reports are handled within one business day on Community management, and daily on Plus. For anything urgent, like a raid or a leaked account, our setup includes an emergency lockdown and a way to reach us. We follow Discord’s rules for reporting serious issues to Discord’s Trust & Safety team.

Will you grow our member count?

We make the server worth joining and staying in: clear onboarding, regular events and a friendly, safe space. How many people join depends on your game, stream or brand. We never buy members or use fake accounts.

Can you post in our voice?

Yes. At kickoff we agree tone, words to use and avoid, and what needs your approval first. Big announcements always go past you before they’re posted.

What kind of events do you run?

Whatever suits your community: game nights, tournaments, Q&As with your devs or you, watch parties, art contests and giveaways. Giveaways follow Discord’s rules and the laws that apply to prizes, and you provide any prizes.

What’s in the monthly report?

Members joined and left, how active the server was, events and how they went, issues handled, what members are asking for, and what we’ll try next month. It’s posted in your client portal.

Do you do other platforms?

Our plans are built around Discord. If you also need help with Reddit, a forum or social posting, tell us and we’ll quote it.

How do I cancel?

Online in your client portal under Plans & billing, in two clicks. The plan ends when the month you’ve paid for runs out, and we remove our access to your server.

What it needs

What a home service website has to get right.

None of this is fancy. It’s the stuff that decides whether a stressed homeowner calls you or the next result.

A call button that never movesOn a phone, your number stays at the bottom of the screen on every page, one tap away. People looking for a repair are often on their phone, standing next to the problem.
A page for each area you serveA real section for each town or county you cover, with what you do there. It helps Google match you to “near me” searches and tells people straight away that you come to them.
Services spelled outRepair, replacement, maintenance plans, emergency calls: each with a plain description, so people searching for that exact thing find it and know you do it.
Proof you’re legitYour license and insurance, how long you’ve been in business, your team, and your real Google reviews linked from the site. We never write or invent reviews.
A quote form for the non-emergenciesNot everyone wants to call. A short form for estimates, with room for a photo of the problem, lands straight in your inbox.
Calls and forms countedSite Stats shows how many people tapped to call or sent a form, and where they came from, so you know whether the site and any ads are paying for themselves.

Questions

Good to know.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

Maybe not. Run it through the free website checkup first. If it works on phones and the problems are small, the care plan ({{price:care-plan}}) can fix them and keep it running. If it isn’t set up for phones or makes people hunt for your number, a rebuild usually pays for itself faster.

Can you write the words for me?

Yes. Tell us what you do, where you work and what makes you different, and we write it in plain language. Every package includes polishing what you give us; full copywriting for a page is an add-on at {{price:add-copy}}.

How long until it’s live?

About three weeks for the Starter website and four to five for the Business website, once we have your details. You see and approve drafts along the way, and nothing goes live until you say so.

Will it get me to the top of Google?

Nobody can honestly promise that. What we do is build the page the way Google expects (fast, clear, a page for each service and area, your business details marked up) and connect it to your Google Business Profile. Ranking also depends on your reviews, how long you’ve been around and your competition. If you need calls this week, ads are the faster route.

Do I own it?

Yes. Once it’s paid for, the site, the words and the images we made are yours, and your domain and hosting are in your own name.

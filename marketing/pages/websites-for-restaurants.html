What it needs

What a restaurant website has to get right.

The menu as real text, not a PDFA PDF is hard to read on a phone and invisible to Google. A text menu loads instantly, can be searched (“gluten-free pizza near me”) and is easy to update when prices change.
Hours and address on the first screenWith a tap for directions and a tap to call. Holiday hours too, kept in step with your Google listing so they never disagree.
Ordering where you already take itButtons straight to your online ordering, delivery apps or reservation system, so the site sends customers to the tools you already use instead of adding another.
Order-ahead and cateringA form for big orders, cakes, trays or catering that lands in your inbox with the date, headcount and details you need.
Photos that make people hungryYour own food, your own room. Food sells on the picture, so the photos get the space.
Allergen notes people can findA clear line on allergens and dietary options on the menu itself, so guests with allergies don’t have to guess or call.

Questions

Good to know.

Can I update the menu myself?

On the Business website and Online store, yes, you get editing access. On the One-page and Starter websites, send us changes and we make them; the care plan ({{price:care-plan}}) includes an hour of edits every month, which covers most menu updates.

Do you set up online ordering?

We link to the ordering, delivery or reservation service you already use and make the buttons hard to miss. We don’t replace your point-of-sale or take a cut of orders.

How fast can it be live?

About two weeks for a one-page website, three for the Starter, once we have your menu, hours and photos. Need it sooner for an opening? Rush delivery is {{price:add-rush}}.

We only have an Instagram. Do we need a website?

Instagram is great for showing what’s new, but it doesn’t show up well in Google searches, isn’t easy to read hours or a menu on, and you don’t own it. A simple site gives you a home people can find, and you can still post the daily specials on Instagram.

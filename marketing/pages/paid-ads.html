Ads questions

How much should I spend on ads?

For most local businesses, $500–$1,500 a month in ad spend is a good start on one platform. We’ll recommend a budget on the kickoff call, and you can change it any time.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Plans renew monthly and you can cancel online any time with 30 days’ notice.

Do you make the ads?

Yes. We write the copy and make new image and short-video creative each month from your photos, videos and offers. The all-platform plan includes short video for TikTok and Reels.

Can I switch platforms later?

Yes. Tell us and we’ll move your plan at the start of the next month.

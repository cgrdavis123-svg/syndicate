Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. There’s no sign-up and no card. If you’d like the report by email, you can leave your name and email under it, and we may follow up once to see if you want help. Every fix the report suggests has a fixed, published price, and plenty of them you can do yourself.

What does it not check?

It’s automated and looks at your home page only. It can’t judge your design, your writing or how well the site sells, and it doesn’t log in or look for private files. For a person to go through the whole site with you, the website & SEO audit is {{price:svc-site-audit}} and comes with a recorded walkthrough and a written plan.

Will it slow down or break my site?

No. It loads your home page, a couple of standard files (robots.txt and sitemap.xml) and up to 40 of your links, once. That’s less than a few visitors browsing.

Can I check a site that isn’t mine?

You can check any public website, the same way anyone can visit it. The report only shows what any visitor or search engine can already see.

Who can see my report?

Anyone with the report’s link, so share it with your web person if you like. We keep reports for six months unless you ask for yours by email. Reports don’t include your name or email. More in our privacy policy.

What’s the score?

It’s worked out from the checks in the report: each one that passes counts fully, a warning counts half and a problem counts nothing, and the most serious ones (like Google being blocked or no secure connection) count double. It’s a quick way to see progress, not a grade on your business.

Buying questions

How do I pay?

By card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), Apple Pay or Google Pay, on Stripe’s secure checkout page. We never see or store your card number.

Can I combine a website and an ads plan?

Yes. Add both to your cart. You pay the website and the first month of ads today, and the ads plan renews monthly after that.

Do you offer discounts?

If you have a promo code, you can enter it on the payment page.

Do I get an invoice?

Yes. Stripe emails a receipt right away, and the paid invoice is in your client portal.

What’s your refund policy?

If we haven’t started work, you get a full refund. Read the details in our terms of sale.

Not sure what you need?

Tell us about your business and we’ll recommend a package, usually within one business day.

The brief

The problem to solve

A small bakery that sells out by noon. Most customers find it on Google Maps or Instagram, but there’s no way to order ahead, and catering requests get lost in DMs.

Our approach
  • One page, ordered the way customers decide: what’s fresh today, when you’re open, how to order ahead.
  • A pickup order button and a catering form above the fold on phones.
  • Big, warm type and real prices, so it reads like the chalkboard menu inside the shop.
What we built

The website

  • One-page site, mobile first
  • Today’s menu with prices
  • Order-ahead and catering forms
  • Hours, map and directions
  • Site Stats goals for calls, orders and catering

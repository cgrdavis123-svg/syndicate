Website questions

Do I need a domain already?

No. If you don’t have one, we’ll help you register it in your own name during kickoff. It usually costs $10–$20 a year, paid to the registrar.

What platform do you build on?

Whatever fits the job. Simple sites are hand-built for speed. Sites you want to edit yourself use an easy editor, and stores use a proven e-commerce platform. We’ll recommend one on the kickoff call.

Can I make changes after launch?

Yes. Business and store sites can be edited by you. For anything else, the care plan includes an hour of edits every month, requested and tracked in your client portal, or we can quote larger changes.

What if I don’t like the design?

Every package includes rounds of changes, and you approve each draft in your portal before we move on. If we can’t get it right before work starts in earnest, see our refund terms.

How fast can you launch?

The times above are typical once we have your content. Add rush delivery to launch in about half the time.

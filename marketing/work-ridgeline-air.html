The brief

The problem to solve

A family HVAC company competing with national brands on Google. When the AC breaks, people call the first trustworthy number they see, so every second on the site matters.

Our approach
  • A tap-to-call button that follows you down the page on phones, and same-day availability shown up top.
  • A section for each service so Google can match “AC repair” and “furnace install” searches.
  • Financing and service area answered before anyone has to ask.
What we built

The website

  • Home, Services, Financing, Service area, Contact
  • Sticky click-to-call on mobile
  • Quote request form
  • Service-area page with a section for each town
  • Call and form tracking in Site Stats

Shipping & returns.

Example policy for this concept store.

How much is shipping?

Standard shipping is $7, or free on orders over $60. Express is $14. Bundles always ship free.

When will it arrive?

Orders ship in 1–2 business days. Standard takes 3–5 business days, express takes 2.

Can I return something?

Unburned candles and unwashed throws can be returned within 30 days for a refund.

It arrived broken

Send a photo within 7 days and we’ll send a replacement, no need to return it.

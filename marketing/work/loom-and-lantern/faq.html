Questions, answered.

Are your candles safe for pets?

We use soy wax and phthalate-free fragrance, but always burn candles in a ventilated room and out of reach of pets.

Do you do wholesale?

Yes, for shops and cafés. Send us a note through the contact page.

How does the refill program work?

Return five clean jars with the prepaid label from your account and your next 8 oz candle is free.

Can I gift wrap?

Yes. Add a note at checkout and we’ll wrap it in recycled paper.

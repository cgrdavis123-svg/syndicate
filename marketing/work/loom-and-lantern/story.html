Started at a market stall.

Loom & Lantern is a fictional shop made for our portfolio. Here’s the story it would tell.

It began with a folding table at a Saturday market and twelve candles poured in a kitchen. People kept coming back for Fireside, then asking if we shipped. Now we pour about 200 at a time in a small studio, still by hand, still testing every batch in our own living room first.

The throws came later: a small mill two states away weaves them for us in runs of fifty. Everything we make is meant for slow evenings at home.

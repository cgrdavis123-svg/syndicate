How we make games

Small team, long runway

We fund our games with what our last game earned and a small amount of outside funding, so we can take the time a game needs.

  • Four-day weeks, no crunch, ever
  • Players see the game early through open playtests
  • Accessibility built in from the first prototype
  • Everyone gets credited, by name, in every game

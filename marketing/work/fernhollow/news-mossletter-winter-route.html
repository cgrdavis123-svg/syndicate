Mossletter 1.3: the Winter Route

Mossletter · September 2 · by the Fernhollow team

The village is getting colder. Mossletter’s free Winter Route update adds a new end-of-year chapter to the game.

What’s new

20 new puzzles built around snow tiles, which slide when you place them next to each other. Six new letters that pick up where some of the neighbors’ stories left off. A warmer coat for the snail.

Fixes

Controller cursor no longer gets stuck on the edge of the board. Undo now works after a delivery. Colorblind palettes cover the new tiles.

How to get it

It downloads automatically. If you finished the main game, the Winter Route opens from the village map.

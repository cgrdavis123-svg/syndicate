Lampwright playtest sign-ups are open

Lampwright · October 14 · by the Fernhollow team

We’ve been playing Lampwright on our own for two years. It’s time to see what happens when other people get their hands on it.

What’s in the playtest

About two hours of the game: the first three districts of Varrow, the first five kinds of glass and a full night from dusk to dawn. Solo and co-op are both open, online and on one screen.

How to get a key

Sign up on the Lampwright site and tell us how you like to play. We’ll send keys by email in waves, starting with the Discord. Your saves won’t carry over to launch, and that’s on purpose: we’ll be changing a lot.

What we want from you

Honest reports. If something is confusing, unfair or boring, we want to hear it. There’s a feedback form in the game and a bug channel in the Discord, and Lu reads every one.

Short description

Lampwright is a co-op roguelite for 1–4 players about the last lampwright in a city where the night is alive. Craft lanterns from salvaged glass, light the streets, and hold out until dawn.

Long description

In the city of Varrow, the dark has learned to move. It creeps up the streets nobody lights and takes what it finds. You are the last person who knows how to make light that lasts.

Each run is one long night in a city that rebuilds itself every time. Salvage glass, craft lanterns with strange properties, and decide which districts to save before your oil runs out. Play alone or with up to three friends online or on one screen, sharing oil and chaining lanterns into walls of light.

Features

  • Build lanterns from salvage
  • Win the city back, street by street
  • Keep the dark at bay until dawn
  • Play solo or with up to 4 friends
  • Five kinds of glass, and what happens when you combine them
  • Streamer-friendly: monetize videos freely

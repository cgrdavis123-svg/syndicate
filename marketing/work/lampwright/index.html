How it plays

Craft. Light. Hold. Together.

Four ideas the whole game is built on.

Craft

Build lanterns from salvage

Every pane of glass you find changes what a lantern does.

Light

Win the city back, street by street

Each run is one long night across a city that changes every time.

Hold

Keep the dark at bay until dawn

The night learns which streets you light, and it comes for the ones you forget.

Together

Play solo or with up to 4 friends

Drop-in online co-op or shared-screen on one couch.

More about the game
Better with friends

The more lanterns, the safer the street

Up to four lampwrights, online or on one screen. Share oil, combine glass and cover each other’s backs when the bell rings.

  • Drop-in, drop-out online co-op
  • Shared-screen couch co-op on one PC
  • Solo runs scale so they’re fair
  • Controller and keyboard, fully remappable

Home/FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things patients ask us most. Don’t see yours? Call or send us a message.

Do you have evening or weekend hours?

Evenings until 7pm on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and one Saturday a month for cleanings.

How often should I get a cleaning?

Every six months for most people. Some patients with gum disease come every three or four months.

Are X-rays safe?

Our digital X-rays use a very small dose of radiation, and we only take them when we need them.

Can I cancel or move my appointment online?

Yes, from the link in your confirmation text. Please give us 24 hours’ notice when you can.

Do you offer sedation?

Nitrous oxide is available for nervous patients. We’ll talk through options at your first visit.

What if I need a specialist?

We refer to trusted local specialists for things like root canals on back teeth, implants and braces.

New patients welcome

Same-week visits, evening hours on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

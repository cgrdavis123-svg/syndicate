Common questions

Do you see kids and adults?

Yes. Whole families can book back-to-back appointments, and kids can start from their first tooth.

Can I get a crown in one visit?

Usually, yes. We design and make most crowns in the office while you wait, in about two hours.

What if I’m nervous about the dentist?

Tell us when you book. We’ll go slowly, explain each step and agree on a stop signal. Nitrous oxide is available.

New patients welcome

Same-week visits, evening hours on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

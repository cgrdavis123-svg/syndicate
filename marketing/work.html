About these projects

Are these real businesses?

No. Every business on this page is fictional, and each demo site says so at the top. They show the design, writing and ad work you’d get, without borrowing anyone else’s results.

Why are there no results numbers?

Because no ads ran for these concepts, and we won’t make numbers up. Each case study shows a campaign plan instead. On real projects, your results come straight from your ad accounts and your Site Stats dashboard.

Can I get a site like one of these?

Yes. Each concept is labeled with the package it fits. Add that package to your cart, or tell us what you have in mind.

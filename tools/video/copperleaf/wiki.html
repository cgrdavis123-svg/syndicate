Land claims

Protect your base so only you and the players you trust can build or open chests there. Claims cost nothing and grow as you play.

Claiming your first area

Hold a golden shovel and right-click two opposite corners of the area you want. Your first claim can be up to 100 blocks across.

COMMANDWHAT IT DOES
/claimClaims a square around where you stand.
/trust <player>Lets a friend build in your claim.
/untrust <player>Takes that permission away.
/claimslistShows all your claims and blocks left.
TIPYou earn more claim blocks every hour you play. Check how many you have with /claimslist.

Abandoned claims

Claims from players who haven't logged in for 60 days are cleared before each new season.
